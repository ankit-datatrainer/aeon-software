Our Journey

From a six-person studio to a global engineering partner.

2010
  2. 2010

    Founded in Cyber City, Gurugram

    Aeon starts life as a six-person product engineering studio building web platforms for Indian start-ups.

  3. 2013

    Mobile & enterprise practice

    Our first enterprise engagements and a dedicated iOS & Android team take us beyond the web.

  4. 2016

    Bengaluru & Hyderabad centres

    Two new delivery centres and our hundredth engineer — with QA automation and UI/UX studios in-house.
